Part A — Privacy Policy

1. Privacy at a glance

Your saved web pages, imported books and documents, highlights, notes, tags, and reading preferences are primarily stored on your device. You do not need a WaveStash account. We do not sell your personal information or use advertising identifiers for targeted advertising. WaveStash does, however, use a limited developer-operated reporting service: it automatically sends a one-time installation event and sends problem reports when you choose to submit them. Those events are relayed to Discord as described below.

2. Information stored locally

  • Saved web pages, downloaded text and images, imported EPUB/PDF and other supported files;
  • Highlights, notes, tags, bookmarks, reading progress, and library organization;
  • Appearance, language, audio, and other preferences;
  • Purchase entitlement and locally maintained feature-allowance information.

We do not routinely upload your reading library, imported documents, highlights, or notes to our reporting service. Your device or operating-system backup provider may handle copies under its own settings and policies.

3. Automatic installation notification

On first launch, WaveStash automatically sends a limited installation event to a developer-operated Cloudflare Worker, which forwards the event to a Discord channel used by Digital Coast Software. The event contains the app version name, app version code, and a timestamp. This helps us understand new installations and version adoption. The event does not intentionally include your name, email address, advertising ID, reading history, notes, or saved content.

As with ordinary internet communications, Cloudflare and potentially other network intermediaries may process technical connection data such as your IP address, request timing, and headers. Discord processes the forwarded event under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not use the installation event for targeted advertising or individual reading-behavior profiling. The installation event is automatic and is not conditional on submitting a problem report.

4. Optional problem reports

When you choose to submit a problem report, WaveStash may send the associated page URL, selected issue category, your written description, app version, and relevant extraction or diagnostic details to our Cloudflare Worker for delivery to Discord. A fallback may open your email app with a draft instead. The report feature is not intended to transmit the full article or your complete library. However, a URL or a note you submit may itself reveal personal or sensitive information, so please review it before sending and avoid including unnecessary personal details.

Reports are used to investigate and improve WaveStash. They may remain in Discord or operational service logs until deleted according to the applicable service settings and our operational practices. We do not promise a fixed deletion period that we cannot verify. Contact us if you wish to request deletion of a report; we will review and act on requests as required by applicable law and subject to our ability to identify the report.

5. Other circumstances in which information leaves your device

  • Web content: When you retrieve a page or online book, the publisher, download host, or content provider receives a network request and may process your IP address and other standard request data under its policies.
  • Google Play: Google processes purchases, billing, and certain app distribution and diagnostic information under its own policies. We do not receive your payment-card details. WaveStash checks purchase entitlement using Google Play services.
  • Device text-to-speech voices: On-device voices process the text being read aloud locally. If you select a network voice through your device’s text-to-speech engine, or the engine uses a network voice because an on-device voice is unavailable, the text being read aloud may be transmitted to that engine’s provider for speech generation. The provider’s terms and privacy policy apply. Voice availability and network behavior depend on your device, installed speech engine, and voice settings.
  • Optional downloads: Downloading enhanced voice models or other optional resources contacts the relevant download host, which may process ordinary connection metadata.
  • Device backup: Your Android or other backup provider may back up eligible application data depending on device settings. Sensitive keys may be excluded from backup.

6. On-device processing

Supported on-device speech synthesis and on-device AI functions process selected content locally during ordinary use and do not require us to receive that content. However, Android system text-to-speech engines may use network voices, including when no suitable on-device voice is available; in those cases, text being spoken may be sent to the speech-engine provider as described in Section 5. Availability and processing behavior depend on device compatibility, installed engines and models, voice settings, and app version.

7. Permissions and security

WaveStash uses internet access for content retrieval, downloads, purchases, and reporting; notifications and foreground-service capabilities for applicable playback and background tasks; and other permissions necessary for supported functions. We use HTTPS for the developer-operated reporting relay and take reasonable steps to limit access to information we receive. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Retention and your choices

You can manage or delete local library content in WaveStash and remove local app data through your device. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local app storage, subject to device backups or copies you exported. Deleting local data does not automatically delete an installation event or problem report already sent to our reporting services. For questions or privacy requests concerning information we control, contact us at the address above. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children

WaveStash is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a report, contact us.

10. Service providers and international processing

Cloudflare provides the developer-operated relay infrastructure; Discord receives developer notifications and problem reports; Google provides app distribution and billing. These companies may process data in countries outside your province or country, subject to their own policies and applicable law. For more information, consult their published privacy policies.

11. Privacy policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as WaveStash changes. We will publish a revised effective date and provide additional notice or obtain consent when required by applicable law. A material change does not retroactively authorize data processing that legally requires new consent.
