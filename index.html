Privacy Policy — WaveStash

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Digital Coast Software ("we", "us", "our") publishes WaveStash, a local-first read-it-later app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and what leaves your device.

1. The short version

WaveStash stores your articles, highlights, notes, tags, and settings on your device. We require no account, operate no servers for the app, show no ads, and use no analytics or tracking.

Information leaves your device only when:

  • (a) the app downloads an article from a publisher's website at your request;
  • (b) you make a purchase through Google Play;
  • (c) you optionally use a third-party voice service with your own API key; or
  • (d) you choose to send us a problem report.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account, identity, or contact information. There is no sign-up and no login.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, advertising identifiers, or behavioral profiling.
  • No in-app crash-reporting SDKs. Note: if you installed WaveStash through Google Play, Google may collect anonymized crash and performance diagnostics under Google's own privacy policy. That collection is by Google, not by us.

3. Information stored on your device

The following is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage:

  • Saved articles, including downloaded article text and images for offline reading;
  • Highlights, notes, and tags;
  • Reading preferences (theme, fonts, display settings);
  • Your Pro purchase entitlement status (whether Pro features are unlocked).

We operate no servers that receive or store this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the device (subject to your device's own backup behavior — see §8).

4. When information leaves your device

(a) Downloading articles. When you save a link, WaveStash fetches the article directly from the publisher's website so you can read it offline. That website receives a standard web request — much like visiting it in a browser — which includes your IP address. The publisher's privacy policy applies to that request, not ours.

(b) Purchases. WaveStash Pro is sold as a one-time purchase through Google Play. Payment information is processed by Google under the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. We never see, handle, or store your card or payment details. The app stores only the resulting entitlement (Pro active or not) locally on your device.

(c) Optional third-party voice service. The built-in on-device voices process everything locally and transmit nothing. If you choose to enter your own API key for a third-party speech provider (for example, LemonFox), the text you choose to listen to is sent to that provider to generate audio, and that provider's privacy policy applies to the transmission. Your API key is encrypted in the device keystore, is excluded from device backups, and must be re-entered if you reinstall the app.

(d) Problem reports. If you tap "Report a problem" on an article, the app sends: the article URL, the issue category you selected, any note you typed, the app version, and technical details about the extraction. It does not send the full article text. Reports go to the developer for debugging. Please do not include personal information in the note field.

5. On-device AI and speech processing

Where supported, AI-generated article summaries are produced entirely on-device. Article text used to generate a summary does not leave your phone. On-device text-to-speech likewise processes locally with no transmission.

6. App permissions

WaveStash requests only the permissions it needs to function:

  • Network access — to download articles you save.
  • Notifications and foreground service — active only while Listen (audio playback) is playing, to provide playback controls.

7. Your control and data retention

You can delete articles, highlights, notes, and tags at any time within the app, and you can export your articles and annotations. Local data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. Because we keep no server-side copy, we cannot recover data you delete.

8. Device backups

Your device's own backup service (for example, Google backup) may include app data according to your system backup settings. Third-party API keys you enter are excluded from backups. You can manage backup behavior in your device's system settings.

9. Children

WaveStash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Third-party content and publishers

Articles displayed in WaveStash belong to their respective publishers. We do not create, endorse, or verify article content. Extraction and summarization are automated processes that may be incomplete, inaccurate, or out of date; AI summaries are provided for convenience only — verify important information against the original source. WaveStash does not circumvent paywalls, login walls, DRM, or other publisher access controls. Your use of publisher content remains subject to those publishers' terms.
